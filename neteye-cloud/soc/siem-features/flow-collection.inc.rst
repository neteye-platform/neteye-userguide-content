
sFlow / Netflow
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If flow collection (sFlow / Netflow) is also envisaged in the project, an `NBOX hardware probe <https://www.ntop.org/products/nbox-appliances/nbox-netflow/>`__
is also installed within the customer's infrastructure, with the objective of collecting sFlow/NetFlow flows
generated by network devices compatible with these protocols,
or (not necessarily alternatively) to collect the mirrored traffic from a series of source network
interfaces.

The technology used in this context is that of `NTOP <https://www.ntop.org/>`__, which incorporates
a series of `behavioral checks <https://www.ntop.org/guides/ntopng/user_interface/shared/alerts/others/flow_checks.html>`__ capable of identifying
a large number of attacks such as exfiltration scenarios through DNS or ICMP tunnelling,
ARP Poisoning, DHCP Starvation, SQL Injection, Cross Site Scripting and many others.

Behavioral checks, when triggered by the collected traffic, generate alerts that are sent
to the NetEye SIEM, on which the corresponding detection rules are active. This can then lead
the SOC analyst, if necessary, to open a ticket to the client.
