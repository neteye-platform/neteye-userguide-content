
When monitoring your infrastructure, utilizing and automating the process of data visualization
proves to be a significant boost in analysis efficiency, and helps you easily obtain the insights
into the state of your infrastructure.

With that in mind, NetEye offers a set of tools for visualizing your data.
This includes both internally developed solutions, like dashboards or monitoring overviews or maps,
and integrated software,e.g. Nagvis.

The :ref:`monitor-visual-dashboard` can be defined and customized to show the real time status
of your infrastructure.

In order to quickly check the monitoring status, inspect :ref:`monitoringview-module`,
that will give you all the needed information about your hosts and services, groups and even scheduled downtimes.
On top of that, you can also customize your monitoring view to reflect only the problems detected.

The :ref:`Geo Map module <geomap-visualizer>` lets you monitor your hosts from a geographical perspective
overlaid on maps from the OpenStreetMap open source initiative.
All of the standard map interface tools are there, such as zooming, panning and markers.
Markers are linked directly to monitoring views.

Integration with :ref:`NagVis <nagvis-overview>` allows you to use overlaid maps to graphically see which network channels
are operating within parameters for those organizations that have multiple physical locations.

With the help of Nagvis integration you will be able to get the display of single Hosts and Services,
or display the summary state of a Host and all its services.
It also provides the ability of visualization of complete IT processes using self drawn graphics.
