About SATAYO
~~~~~~~~~~~~

**SATAYO** is a cloud-based cybersecurity service that provides organizations
with a clear view of their digital presence from an external perspective.
By correlating information from multiple data sources, SATAYO builds an
accurate representation of what is publicly visible about an organization
on the Internet—similar to what a potential attacker could observe.

The service collects and analyzes data exclusively from
**Open-Source Intelligence (OSINT)** sources, meaning all information used by
SATAYO is publicly available. Through this analysis, SATAYO identifies exposed
assets, potential vulnerabilities, and possible attack vectors that could be
exploited by cybercriminals. This external visibility helps organizations
better understand their risk exposure and take informed actions to improve
their security posture.

As a fully cloud-based solution, SATAYO operates continuously and is designed
for modern, dynamic environments where domains, cloud services, and
internet-facing systems frequently change.

.. _easm_with_satayo:

External Visibility with SATAYO
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

SATAYO, offered as a service by NetEye.Cloud, helps organizations manage their external
attack surface. It provides continuous, outside-in visibility of all publicly exposed assets,
supporting the discovery, monitoring, and reduction of potential security risks — a core goal
of *External Attack Surface Management (EASM)*.

**EASM** focuses on discovering, monitoring,
and reducing an organization’s publicly exposed assets and associated risks.
SATAYO supports this approach by providing continuous, outside-in visibility
into the organization’s external attack surface.

SATAYO enables organizations to discover and inventory internet-facing assets
associated with their identity, including systems and services that may not be
centrally managed or fully documented. By continuously monitoring these
assets, the service detects changes in the external attack surface, such as
newly exposed services, configuration changes, or emerging weaknesses.

By analyzing exposure from an attacker’s perspective, SATAYO helps
organizations identify real-world risks that are visible and potentially
exploitable. This outside-in approach supports effective risk prioritization,
allowing security teams to focus remediation efforts on the most relevant
exposures.

SATAYO’s cloud-native architecture ensures simplicity and scalability. No
on-premises deployment is required, and the service naturally scales with
cloud, hybrid, and rapidly evolving environments, making it well suited for
organizations seeking continuous EASM coverage with minimal operational
overhead.
