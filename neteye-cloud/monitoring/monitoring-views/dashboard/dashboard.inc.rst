Dashboard
~~~~~~~~~

The **Dashboard** area gives you a flexible workspace where you can organize the monitoring
information that is most relevant to your daily work. It is designed for comfort and
efficiency: instead of opening several monitoring views one by one, you can collect
the most important views, summaries, and widgets in one place and arrange them
according to your preferred workflow.

For |nec| users the Dashboard is typically pre-configured, so you can immediately start
from a standard set of dashlets that provide commonly used monitoring information.

.. figure:: /neteye-cloud/monitoring/img/dashboard.png

   The Dashboard for current incidents

You can still adapt the Dashboard to your own needs.
Choose the monitoring views you use most often and add them either as a dedicated dashboard
or as a dashlet inside one of your existing dashboards. This helps you create different
workspaces for different purposes, such as a general operational overview, focused host
or service monitoring, troubleshooting, or team-specific monitoring responsibilities.

In practice, the dashboard helps you personalize how you consume monitoring data.
Standard dashlets provide a ready-to-use starting point, while configurable views and
additional dashlets let you keep the most relevant information visible and reduce
the time you need to navigate between monitoring pages.

For explicit information on how to tune your dashboards and dashlets please see the :ref:`dedicated section <neteye-dashboard>` of the |NE| guide.
